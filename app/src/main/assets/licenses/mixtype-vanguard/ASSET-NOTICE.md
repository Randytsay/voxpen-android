# MixType / VanguardLexicon Traditional Chinese dictionary

VoxPen bundles the unchanged Traditional Chinese source files from the
`vChewing-VanguardLexicon` dependency pinned as version `4.8.4` by MixType. The
compressed archive is `assets/mixtype-vanguard-cht-4.8.4.zip`. VoxPen parses these
files locally to build its Pinyin and initials indexes; it does not bundle the
macOS Swift input engine.

The upstream project describes its source material and licensing in the adjacent
`README.MD`, `LICENSE.txt`, and `LICENSE-CHT.txt`. The corpus includes material
from the Taiwan Ministry of Education dictionary (CC BY-ND 3.0), CNS11643 / the
National Characters standard, LibTaBE (3-Clause BSD), Singapore Mandarin
resources, and VanguardLexicon-maintained entries (Mulan PSL-2.0). Retain the
source-specific terms and attribution in the upstream README when redistributing
this app or its dictionary asset.

The embedded archive contains the nine `phrases-*-cht.txt` files plus
`char-kanji-core.txt`, `char-kanji-cns.txt`, and `char-kanji-gbex.txt` from the
pinned dependency. Files inside the archive are included verbatim.
